💡 Queue Principles in Order Processing

  • Fairness Guarantee: Orders are prepared in the exact chronological arrival order. No newer order can bypass an earlier order.
  • Circular Buffer Optimization: Eliminates the costly O(N) shifting overhead of basic linear arrays by wrapping pointers using (index + 1) % capacity.
  • Underflow Protection: Guards against attempting to cook or dequeue when zero orders are waiting.
  • Overflow Protection: Prevents buffer overflow when the restaurant kitchen hits maximum queue capacity.
